**Quiz-me gate (verifier → human):**
1. **Design:** Why enqueue on `turn/end` instead of immediately on `assistant/message`?
2. **Edge case:** What happens when a turn completes with only plugin-injected user content?
3. **Impact:** Why is `void enqueueRemember(...)` required on the `session/event` hot path?

**Suggested answers (for human confirmation):**
1. One evaluate per turn needs the full turn boundary; `turn/end` guarantees the assistant step finished and avoids duplicate enqueues on multi-step tool loops until the turn closes.
2. `isDurableUserMessage` rejects plugin sources → no user text in buffer → `buildSaveInput` returns undefined → no evaluate call.
3. DSH must not block the reply path on Remex write-gate latency; fire-and-forget keeps evaluate async while errors are handled in the background promise.

- Skips plugin injects and non-completed turns
- `void enqueueRemember(...)` — hot path never awaits job poll
- UUID v5 `source_turn_ids` via `messageIdToTurnUuid`
